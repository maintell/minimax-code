Billing System Upgrade & Asset Migration

MiniMax has upgraded its billing system. Agent and the Open Platform are now fully integrated: Token Plan subscriptions can be used directly with MiniMax Agent, and Agent Credits have been unified into MiniMax Credits.

Any remaining Credits in your account have been converted to new MiniMax Credits at their original purchase value. Active subscribers will also receive additional rewards. The full migration details are below.

Personal Plans (Monthly)

Agent subscription with auto-renew enabled

Your remaining Credits have been converted to new MiniMax Credits at their original purchase value, plus a 1,000 Credit bonus.

  • With an active Token Plan: You'll receive bonus Credits equivalent to a Token Plan monthly subscription matched to your Agent Plan tier.
  • Without a Token Plan: Your Agent subscription has been canceled. You've received a complimentary 1-month Token Plan matched to your previous Agent Plan tier, which will auto-renew at that tier starting next month.

Mobile app subscriptions: If you originally subscribed through the iOS or Android app, your remaining Credits have been converted at their original purchase value with a 1,000 Credit bonus. Your Agent subscription has been canceled, and you've received a complimentary 1-month Token Plan matched to your previous tier. This Token Plan will not auto-renew and will expire after one month. To continue, please purchase Token Plan on the web.

Agent subscription with auto-renew disabled

Your remaining Credits have been converted to new MiniMax Credits at their original purchase value, plus a 1,000 Credit bonus. The rest depends on your Token Plan status:

  • With an active Token Plan: You'll receive bonus Credits equivalent to a Token Plan monthly subscription matched to your Agent Plan tier.
  • Without a Token Plan: Your Agent subscription has been canceled. You've received a complimentary 1-month Token Plan matched to your previous Agent Plan tier. This Token Plan will not auto-renew and will expire after one month.

Personal Plans (Annual)

For annual subscribers, your remaining Credits are first converted to new MiniMax Credits at their original purchase value. The rest of the migration depends on your Token Plan status and payment method:

No active Token Plan and not subscribed via mobile app

Your account has been upgraded to a matching annual Token Plan, as shown below. The new subscription's auto-renew setting mirrors your original Agent subscription: if auto-renew was enabled, it stays enabled; if it was disabled, the new subscription will not auto-renew and will expire after 1 year.

Current Agent Annual Plan Migration & Reward
Basic ($192/year) Upgraded to the Plus annual Token Plan ($200/year), plus 1 month's worth of bonus Credits.
Pro ($696/year) Upgraded to the Max annual Token Plan ($500/year). The $196 difference has been converted to new Credits, plus 1 month's worth of bonus Credits.
Ultra ($1,308/year) Upgraded to the Max-Highspeed annual Token Plan ($800/year). The $508 difference has been converted to new Credits, plus 1 month's worth of bonus Credits.

With active Token Plan, or subscribed via mobile app

Your current subscription remains unchanged. The unused portion of your annual Agent subscription has been converted to new MiniMax Credits at equivalent value, plus 1 additional month's worth of bonus Credits.

Team Plans

Rewards for team accounts are issued as Token Plan Team subscriptions.

Agent Team subscription with auto-renew enabled

Your team's shared Credits have been converted to new MiniMax Credits at their original purchase value, plus a 3,000 Credit bonus. You've also received a 1-month Starter Token Plan Team (seat count matched to your current team size), with auto-renew enabled.

Agent Team subscription with auto-renew disabled

Your team's shared Credits have been converted to new MiniMax Credits at their original purchase value, plus a 3,000 Credit bonus. You've also received a 1-month Starter Token Plan Team (seat count matched to your current team size). This subscription will not auto-renew and will expire after one month.

Using Your Credits

  • Validity: MiniMax Credits are valid for 1 year from the migration date.
  • Consumption order: When using MiniMax Agent, your Token Plan quota is consumed first. Credits are only used once your Token Plan quota is depleted, or for features not included in your subscription.
  • Cross-platform: Your MiniMax Credits balance works across both MiniMax Agent and the Open Platform API.
